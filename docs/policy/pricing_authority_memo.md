# Pricing Authority Memo (fictional, Lakeshore Group)

Version 1, effective 2026-11-01. The authority policy in `policy/authority.yaml` cites
the sections below. Every rule must point to one of them.

## 1. Who may send a quote
1.1 Only people may approve sending a quote to a customer. Software agents may prepare
    drafts, never approve them.
1.2 Estimators and senior estimators may approve sending a quote, subject to section 2.

## 2. When a senior estimator must approve
2.1 Quoted margin below 18% of price.
2.2 Quote value above $250,000.

## 3. Messages that must not be acted on in the quote desk
3.1 A message with an unresolved warning (a request to change payment details, text
    addressed to the software, an unknown or mismatched sender) is handled outside the
    quote desk. Payment-detail changes are verified by phone, using the number on file.

## 4. Target margins
4.1 Target margin by customer segment: utility 22%, contractor 25%, OEM 20%,
    distributor 18%, other 24%. Quotes are valid for 30 days.
